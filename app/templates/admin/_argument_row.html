{# One argument: the quote, who said it, and where to check it. The three fields carry no index in their names. The browser posts repeated fields in document order, so the nth text belongs with the nth author and the nth source — which is what lets a row be added by cloning this markup and removed by deleting it, with nothing to renumber. See `app.routes.admin.forms.ArgumentRows`. Expects `side` ('for' / 'against'), and `text`, `author`, `source`. #}
{# Removing is emptying: a row whose three fields are blank is skipped on save, so this button needs no server route and no confirmation. #}
